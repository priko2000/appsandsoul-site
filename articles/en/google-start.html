How to appear on Google: first steps and a Maps listing

When someone searches for “a store nearby” or your name, the first thing they see is Google. Here is where to start so they find you there.

David Mushailov, founder of Apps & Soul

Your Google business listing

This is a free business profile (Google Business Profile) shown in search and on Maps: name, hours, phone, website, photos, reviews. For a local business it is often more important than the website itself.

  • It is created at business.google.com.
  • If customers come to you, you list an address. If you go to customers or work remotely, Google requires you to hide the address and shows a service area instead.
  • Google checks that the business is real. These days it often asks for a short video: a sign or the surroundings, tools, the workplace.

How to fill it in so it works

  • The exact name, without extra keywords: Google penalises that.
  • The right business category.
  • Opening hours, including holidays.
  • Real photos: outside, inside, products, team.
  • A link to the website and a call button.

A website Google understands

  • Fast and easy on a phone: Google evaluates a site primarily by its mobile version.
  • Clear page titles: what you sell and where.
  • Structured data that tells Google the business name, address and phone.
  • Useful texts: articles, answers to questions, product descriptions.

Patience

A new site needs time before Google ranks it high. Usually weeks and months, not days. A Maps listing often starts bringing customers sooner.
