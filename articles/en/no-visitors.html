A website but no visitors: why it happens and what to do

A common story: a beautiful site goes live and orders do not grow. A website does not bring people by itself — you have to bring them. Here are the main reasons and what to do about them.

David Mushailov, founder of Apps & Soul

Reason 1: nobody knows about the site

A website is a shop window on a street nobody walks down yet. Tell people about it: in your WhatsApp status, on social media, on business cards, receipts and signage, in your message signature. Send regular customers a personal link.

Reason 2: Google does not know about it

  • Create a Google business listing and add the site to it.
  • Connect the free Google Search Console: it shows whether Google sees your pages and which searches find you.
  • Check that the site is not accidentally hidden from search engines: it happens after development.

Reason 3: people arrive but leave

  • The site opens slowly on a phone.
  • It is unclear what you sell and how to order.
  • No prices or delivery terms.
  • The contact button is hidden.

Simple visit statistics show how many people come and on which page they leave.

Reason 4: nothing to search for

If the site has three pages with one sentence each, Google has nothing to show in search. Useful texts — product descriptions, answers to questions, articles — give it material.

Paid advertising

Ads on Google and social media bring people fast, but only while you pay. Launch them once the site handles visitors well, otherwise you are paying for people who leave.

The order of steps

  1. Make sure the site is convenient and fast.
  2. A Google listing and Search Console.
  3. Tell all your existing customers.
  4. Fill the site with useful content.
  5. Start advertising with a clear budget and check the results.
