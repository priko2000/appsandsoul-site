An AI assistant for a store: what it can and cannot do

People talk about artificial intelligence as if it will replace the whole staff. In reality it is a useful helper with clear limits. Here is an honest look.

David Mushailov, founder of Apps & Soul

What a good assistant can do

  • Answer frequent questions around the clock: opening hours, delivery, stock, prices.
  • Suggest products from a description: “a dry red wine for meat under 100 ₪”.
  • Speak the customer’s language: Hebrew, Russian, English and more.
  • Take an enquiry or order and pass it to you ready.
  • Help the owner: write product descriptions, translate, sort through reviews.

What it cannot (or should not) do

  • Know what it was not given. The assistant answers from your product data and rules. If the data is missing, a good assistant says so and hands the question to a person.
  • Resolve disputes. Complaints, returns, special arrangements — that is your job.
  • Make decisions for you. Discounts, exceptions and responsibility stay with the owner.

The main risk: making things up

Language models sometimes answer confidently with things that are not true. So a business assistant must be constrained: answer only from your data, promise nothing outside your rules, and honestly say “I don’t know”.

Who needs one

Businesses with many similar questions, a large range, or customers who write outside working hours. If you get five questions a day, a good FAQ section on the site may be enough for now.

How to start without risk

At first the assistant only suggests answers and you send them. Once it is clearly accurate, you can let it handle simple questions fully.
