Your own website or a builder like Wix and Shopify

Builders promise a website in one evening, developers promise a site “made for you”. Both are legitimate; the question is what your business actually needs. Here is an honest comparison, no sales pitch.

David Mushailov, founder of Apps & Soul

What a builder does well

  • A quick start: ready-made templates, a site can be up in a few days.
  • Hosting, the secure connection and updates are handled by the platform.
  • Texts and photos are easy to change yourself.

Where a builder starts to get in the way

  • A monthly fee forever. As long as you pay, the site works; stop paying and it goes offline.
  • You cannot take the whole site with you. On many platforms the design and structure stay inside the platform, so moving means rebuilding them.
  • Non-standard tasks. Connecting to your till or stock, your own ordering flow, special delivery logic — these are paid add-ons or simply impossible.
  • Hebrew and several languages. Right-to-left and three languages on one site do not work equally well everywhere.
  • A cut of your sales. On some plans and in some countries the platform charges an extra percentage when you use an external payment provider. Check the terms of your plan.

What your own website gives you

  • It is yours. The code, products and data belong to you, and the site can move to other hosting or another developer.
  • Any feature. The site is built around your process instead of you bending to a template.
  • Room to grow. Start simple, then add an app, a management panel or an assistant.

The downsides, honestly: the start usually costs more, and big changes need a developer. Everyday things such as products, prices and promotions you can change yourself in the management panel.

How to choose

A builder fits if you are testing an idea, have few products and a minimal budget.

Your own site pays off if the website is an important part of the business, you have many products, your processes are non-standard, or you need Hebrew, Russian and English together.
